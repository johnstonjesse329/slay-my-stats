When the other players in a multiplayer run upload it too, the run's Party line names them, and each name opens that player's own copy of the same run. Played with, above the run list, narrows it to the runs you played with the people you pick.

Seeds you played more than once are numbered now: a "#2" by the character in the list, and "Attempt 2 of 3" in the run's header, with links to the other attempts.
