This addition is intended to raise awareness on cool combinations from our run pool and how your run is going per floor, per act, and at completion, like on the site. The intent here is to enrich your run, not drive for you. If I get that balance wrong, please let me know. If you find these additions annoying or unwanted, turn them off in the settings!

The new mod is up on GitHub. Workshop updates are on hold while Mega Crit works through Steam’s temporary manual approval requirement. You can install the mod by hand or keep uploading your runs on the site.

  • Card and relic tips show how often players pick them and how often those runs win. You can see the stats in the Card Compendium and Relic Collection, too.
  • Synergy hints show what a card or relic gives your deck or wants from it, with counts for matching cards in your deck where available.
  • Event choice tips show pick and win rates where we have data.
  • The run-strength indicator in the top bar compares damage taken and turns against past wins. Hover over it for a fight and act breakdown, plus combat numbers like total damage and your biggest hit.
  • The new Slay My Stats tab in Settings lets you toggle each display and pause automatic uploads.
