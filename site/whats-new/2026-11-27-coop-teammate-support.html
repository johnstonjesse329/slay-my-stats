Open a co-op run and look for Teammate support above the fight-by-fight charts. The table shows what each player contributed and adds it up for the team. Pick an act or fight, or turn on Player-to-player breakdown to see who helped whom.

Hover over or tap a heading for an example. Knockdown and Flanking can help a teammate deal more damage, Tag Team can make an Attack play again, and The Ball can change owners after you play it.

One upload with the mod can include support recorded for the whole party. To see delayed draws from cards like Plot and transfers like The Ball, record a new run with Slay My Stats v0.0.8 or later.
