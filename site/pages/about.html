About Slay My Stats

Slay My Stats is a Slay the Spire 2 run history site for questions like “Am I actually getting better at Ironclad?” and “Where did that run go sideways?”

This started when I was hard stuck at A9 on Ironclad. I mean REALLY hard stuck (97 attempts to clear it). I did much better on A10, where it only took me 70 :D

I built Slay My Stats because I wanted to see what my win rate looked like on the way up and how much it dropped when I hit those walls at A9 and A10. And yes, I used AI to help build it. At some point my little local project became a whole site and a mod, and I figured other people might want to use it too.

Cool story, so why another tracker? The difference is where the focus sits: your own gameplay, not a social feed and not a leaderboard for whoever wins the most runs. Fairly deep trends for each character on each ascension, run by run, plus whatever extra the mod records — that’s the bit I think the other platforms are missing.

The folks in Worth watching below are the ones who helped me get better at the game. Watching them play and hearing why they made their picks helped me rethink mine. If you’re stuck where I was, that’s where I’d start.

Poke around your runs

You can see how you’re doing by character and ascension, then open a run and go floor by floor through the fights, cards, relics, and deck changes. In co-op, look for Teammate support. Pick a fight and see who helped who, which is handy when you’re pretty sure your friend did all the work but would like to see the numbers. See an example profile

Getting your runs in

You can sign in with Steam and upload your run history yourself. Your Steam display name and uploaded runs appear on a public profile you can share. Upload your runs

If you’d rather skip the folder picking, the mod uploads each run when it ends and records more detail about your fights. It also shows pick and win rates for cards, relics, and event choices, points out possible synergies, and lets you see how your fights compare to past wins. You can turn those displays off in Settings if you’d rather make your questionable card picks in peace. Get the mod

Under the hood

The site and mod are both on GitHub. If a chart looks wrong or there’s something you wish the site showed you, hit Feedback in the bottom-right corner. You can also send feedback from the mod’s settings, or click the Run Strength icon to report something about the fight you’re in. Site code · Mod code · What’s new
