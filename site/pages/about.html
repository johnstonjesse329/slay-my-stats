About Slay My Stats

Yet another Slay the Spire run history site... Why, you might ask? Why are you even here reading this? Fair questions... And yes, I did use AI to help build this. This started off as a local project to help me understand why I was hard stuck at A9 on Ironclad during my early climb. I mean REALLY hard stuck (97 attempts to clear it). I did much better on A10, where it only took me 70 :D

Cool story, so why again? Well, I won't pretend I'm not reinventing the wheel a bit with how things turned out. This is a passion project that I wanted to see through to the end and make available to others, despite being late to the party. My focus here is highlighting your own gameplay and trends rather than being a social aggregator. You'll be able to see fairly deep trends for each character on each ascension, which I think other platforms out there are missing today.
