Sign in with Steam and upload

Why sign in?

Steam confirms who you are, so nobody else can upload as you. Your profile gets a page to share at slay-my-stats.com/u/yourname.

What's public

Your Steam display name and your runs, on your profile page. You're listed in player search, and your runs count toward the stats across all players.
