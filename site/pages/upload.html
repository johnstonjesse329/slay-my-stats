Upload your runs

Sign in with Steam, then pick your run history folder. Upload again any time; runs already on your profile are skipped.

Sign in with Steam and upload

Why sign in?

Steam confirms who you are, so nobody else can upload as you. Your profile gets a page to share at slay-my-stats.com/u/yourname.

What's public

Your Steam display name and your runs, on your profile page. You're listed in player search, and your runs count toward the stats across all players.

Or let the game do it

Subscribe to the Slay My Stats mod on the Steam Workshop. Each run uploads when it ends, along with the runs you've already played, with no sign-in needed. The mod sends a log of each fight too, which adds more detail to your runs.
