Upload your runs

Your run history lives in the game's saves folder on the PC you play on, so the site can't fetch it — you hand it over. That's a Steam sign-in and one folder pick.

Sign in with Steam and upload

Why sign in?

To keep upload abuse out without running an account system, this bit is offloaded to Steam. The login does a few things: it gates upload abuse a bit, and it makes sure your runs are properly associated with your Steam username, which is also easy to search for. Your runs become a page at slay-my-stats.com/u/yourname you can share.

What happens when you upload

  1. Click the button above and sign in with Steam. Your browser comes back to the site signed in.
  2. Copy the path to your run history folder — the page you land on shows it, with a Copy button.
  3. Click Choose history folder, paste that path, and pick the folder, even though it looks empty (the picker shows folders, not the run files inside).

Your browser reads the run files on your disk and sends them here; nothing leaves your PC until you pick that folder. Upload again any time: runs your profile already has are skipped, so only new ones are added.

What's public

Your profile page, at slay-my-stats.com/u/yourname, is public: your Steam display name and your runs. It's listed in the player search, and its runs count toward the stats across all players. Your Steam ID is only used on the backend to match your uploads to your profile name.
